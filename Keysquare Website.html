How it works

How a typical engagement runs

Most engagements start with a single question. The platform keeps the full trail, so the same analysis can be re-run when the market moves.

STEP 1

Define

Share your network, sales data and objectives. We map them against the right geography, from state to street block.

STEP 2

Fuse

Your data is joined with Keysquare's layers on a common spatial index, cleaned and kept private to your workspace.

STEP 3

Model

Our models score demand, competition and risk for every cell and candidate site, calibrated to your category.

STEP 4

Decide

Review ranked results in the map studio, ask Copilot follow-up questions, or push scores into your systems by API.

Processing infrastructure

Location analysis is computationally heavy. Joining millions of points to millions of cells, routing catchments across whole cities and retraining models on fresh data all demand serious processing power.

Parallel

A dedicated compute platform that processes thousands of spatial operations at the same time

Accelerated

In-memory

Elastic

Company

About Keysquare Technologies

Keysquare Technologies is a location intelligence company headquartered in New Delhi. We combine deep spatial data, artificial intelligence and high-performance computing to turn location into a measurable business advantage.

Our story

Why we built Keysquare

Opening a store, a branch or a warehouse commits capital for years. Yet many of these decisions still rest on site visits, outdated census tables and gut feel. We started Keysquare to change that.

Our platform brings together the data that describes how people live, move and spend, and makes it usable by the teams who make location decisions every day. Because India's markets change quickly, we focus on depth, freshness and detail down to the street block.

Today Keysquare serves businesses across retail, financial services, real estate, consumer goods and the public sector, in India and in international markets.
