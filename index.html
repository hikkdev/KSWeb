How it works

How a typical engagement runs

Most engagements start with a single question. The platform keeps the full trail, so the same analysis can be re-run when the market moves.

STEP 1

Define

Share your network, sales data and objectives. We map them against the right geography, from state to street block.

STEP 2

Fuse

Your data is joined with Keysquare's layers on a common spatial index, cleaned and kept private to your workspace.

STEP 3

Model

Our models score demand, competition and risk for every cell and candidate site, calibrated to your category.

STEP 4

Decide

Review ranked results in the map studio, ask Copilot follow-up questions, or push scores into your systems by API.
