About Keysquare Technologies

Keysquare Technologies is a location intelligence company headquartered in New Delhi. We combine deep spatial data, artificial intelligence and high-performance computing to turn location into a measurable business advantage.

Our story

Why we built Keysquare

Opening a store, a branch or a warehouse commits capital for years. Yet many of these decisions still rest on site visits, outdated census tables and gut feel. We started Keysquare to change that.

Our platform brings together the data that describes how people live, move and spend, and makes it usable by the teams who make location decisions every day. Because India's markets change quickly, we focus on depth, freshness and detail down to the street block.

Today Keysquare serves businesses across retail, financial services, real estate, consumer goods and the public sector, in India and in international markets.
