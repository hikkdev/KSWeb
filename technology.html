Infrastructure

Processing infrastructure

Location analysis is computationally heavy. Joining millions of points to millions of cells, routing catchments across whole cities and retraining models on fresh data all demand serious processing power.

Parallel

A dedicated compute platform that processes thousands of spatial operations at the same time

Accelerated

Model training and inference run on specialised high-throughput hardware

In-memory

Hot data is held close to the processors for interactive map exploration

Elastic

Capacity scales up for national studies and down for daily queries

Trust

Privacy and data security

We work with aggregated, anonymised signals. We never identify or track individuals.

Aggregated by default

Movement and audience data is aggregated to areas and time windows, with minimum thresholds before any value is shown.

Your data stays yours

Client data lives in an isolated workspace, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is never used to serve other clients.

Built for Indian regulation

Our data practices are designed to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, with data hosted in India by default.
